### Summary

For our October meetup, we'll be doing lightning talks 🌩🌩🌩

One of our more popular themes, a lightning-talk event is an intellectual variety show: a series of 7 minute talks! Details below.

### To Present

Contact an Elder in our Slack to sign-up. If the sign-up list fills up, we reserve the right to decline your entry. Aim to have slides available on the web (to avoid swapping computers), but if necessary, we can swap computers. Note that 7 minutes will be a hard-stop, so practice your talk to polish the timing!

We are more lenient than usual in terms of topics: if you are into something nerdy, it is a candidate for a lightning talk. (We are generally accommodating but even more so for lightning talks.)

See you at The Foundry! Please note that we'll have someone at the door until 7:15 pm, but after that it will be important to ping us via DM on [Slack](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScjMRLiiKXqeHCjCSAD37mFxJdH5fskiok-LUaIGtPUZ63glw/viewform).
